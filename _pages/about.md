# **About Me**  

I’m **Tyler Pollard**, a statistician with **6+ years of experience** in 
**Bayesian modeling, predictive analytics, and interactive dashboards**. My passion lies in 
transforming complex data into **actionable insights**—whether that means developing probabilistic 
models, optimizing decision-making, or building dynamic data visualization tools.  

## **Professional Background**  
Originally from **Baltimore, MD**, I earned my **B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Clemson University**, 
where I developed a strong analytical mindset before transitioning into statistics. My curiosity for 
data-driven decision-making led me to the **U.S. Army Evaluation Center**, where I applied advanced 
statistical methods to **lead 24 teams across survivability, vulnerability, effectiveness, and reliability assessments**.  

For my contributions, I was honored with:  
✔ **[The Department of the Army Civilian Service Commendation Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_the_Army_Civilian_Service_Commendation_Medal#:~:text=The%20Department%20of%20the%20Army,the%20military%20Army%20Commendation%20Medal)**  
✔ **AEC Employee of the Quarter Award**  

To deepen my expertise, I pursued a **Master of Statistics at North Carolina State University**, 
focusing on **multilevel modeling, Bayesian inference, time series forecasting, and data science**. Now based in the 
**Tampa/Sarasota area of Florida**, I continue refining my skills in **predictive modeling and data science**, 
tackling projects in **sports analytics, defense applications, and interactive dashboard development**.  

## **Beyond the Numbers**  
Outside of work, I apply the same **analytical mindset** to my personal interests. Whether I’m 
**golfing, strength training, or experimenting with juicing recipes**, I enjoy optimizing 
performance—both in data and in life. I’m also an avid **Baltimore Ravens, Baltimore Orioles, and Clemson Tigers fan**, 
always looking for ways to merge **sports and analytics** in creative ways.
