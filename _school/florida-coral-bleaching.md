This study examines coral bleaching patterns across Florida’s reefs using a 
**Bayesian modeling approach**. By analyzing environmental factors like 
**ocean temperature, storms, and water quality**, it explores trends in 
bleaching severity over time.  

📍 **Key highlights:**  
- Investigates how environmental conditions influence bleaching risk  
- Captures spatial and temporal patterns in bleaching events  
- Identifies key predictors of severe bleaching years
